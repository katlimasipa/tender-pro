## Design Context

### Users
Procurement officers, small to medium enterprise owners, contractors, and freelancers who need to quickly generate professional, legally compliant tender documents, quotes, and invoices on the go. They often work in high-pressure environments where speed and presentation matter.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, organised, and polished. Soft and approachable on the surface, precise underneath — like a beautifully organised planner. Confident without being corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
Soft Pastel Dashboard (per user's reference image, Nov 2026).
- **Theme**: Light mode primary. A blush-pink to lavender gradient page backdrop with crisp white cards floating on it.
- **Typography**: Quicksand for headings and display (rounded, friendly geometric sans), Nunito Sans for body and data.
- **Color**: Blush pink and lavender pastel panels, white surfaces, deep plum-slate ink text, near-black pill buttons, and a rose accent for icons and highlights. No gradients on text.
- **Shape & Space**: Generous rounding everywhere — pill buttons and tabs, rounded-2xl/3xl cards, round icon tiles. Diffuse soft shadows (never hard offset shadows or harsh 1px black borders).
- **Navigation**: Desktop = floating white icon rail on the left (vertically centered, rounded card) + centered pill tab bar at the top with labels (Dashboard, Tenders, Company). Mobile = top bar with drawer.
- **Anti-references**: No hard black borders, no sharp brutalist corners, no safety orange, no serif/industrial-editorial styling, no odd decorative symbols.

### Design Principles
1. **Soft structure**: Cards, pills, and rounded tiles create hierarchy — not heavy borders.
2. **Friendly clarity**: Rounded typography and generous spacing keep dense data approachable.
3. **Pastel with contrast**: Pastel panels for tinted content, near-black pills for primary actions, rose for small accents.
4. **Immediate feedback**: Gentle hover states and color inversions for active navigation.
