## Design Context

### Users
Procurement officers, small to medium enterprise owners, contractors, and freelancers who need to quickly generate professional, legally compliant tender documents, quotes, and invoices on the go. They often work in high-pressure environments where speed and presentation matter.

### Brand Personality
Authoritative, crisp, and no-nonsense. The personality is a fusion of "industrial" and "editorial" — like a well-printed architectural blueprint mixed with a premium financial broadsheet. It is confident without being flashy. 

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial-Editorial Minimalist. 
- **Theme**: Light mode primary, high contrast. We use stark white surfaces with deep, almost-black inks (deep slate/charcoal) rather than generic dark grays.
- **Typography**: A bold, distinctive serif for headings to give an editorial, established feel (e.g., *Instrument Serif* or *Cormorant*), paired with an ultra-clean, technical sans-serif for UI and data (e.g., *Space Grotesk* or *IBM Plex Sans*). 
- **Color**: Monochromatic base (black, white, cream) with one sharp, unapologetic accent color — a highly saturated, deep electric blue or an industrial safety orange. No gradients. No soft pastels. 
- **Borders & Space**: Hard lines, visible 1px borders to delineate sections (like an old-school ledger or technical drawing). Generous whitespace.
- **Anti-references**: No glassmorphism, no soft glowing drop shadows, no "AI" gradients, no rounded bubbbly buttons. Corners are sharp or minimally rounded (2px max). 

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over Decoration**: Every line, border, and color must serve a structural purpose. 
2. **High-Fidelity Typography**: Use typography scale and weight variations to create hierarchy, not just size.
3. **Information Density**: Financial data should be tabular and dense, while actionable areas should be spacious.
4. **Immediate Feedback**: The interface must feel instantly responsive, using subtle color inversions for active states.
